Sweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Sweep is a privacy-first photo cleaner for iPhone. We have nothing to monetize from your data, because Sweep doesn't collect any.

Data we collect

None. Sweep does not transmit photos, metadata, identifiers, or analytics to any server we operate. Everything — duplicate detection, blur scoring, screenshot filtering — happens entirely on your iPhone.

Photo Library access

Sweep asks for full Photo Library access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription). It uses this access to:

  • List photos and videos in your library (Photos framework).
  • Read low-resolution thumbnails to compute perceptual hashes locally.
  • Delete items when you explicitly tap "Delete" — iOS shows its own system confirmation before anything is removed.

No image is uploaded, exported, or shared by Sweep.

In-app purchases

If you buy Sweep Pro ($9.99, one-time), the purchase is processed by Apple's StoreKit. Sweep receives only the entitlement (yes/no Pro), never your payment details. Receipt verification is performed on-device.

Third-party services

None. Sweep contains no SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, crash reporters, or feature flagging libraries.

Children

Sweep is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear here with a new "Last updated" date.
